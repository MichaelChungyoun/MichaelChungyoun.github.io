Therapeutic antibody candidates must exhibit strong developability properties: High conformational stability, high expression levels, low aggregation propensity, low polyreactivity, and low immunogenicity. Accurately predicting developability properties is critical to drug development success. Here, we present FLAb, a benchmark evaluating state-of-the-art protein AI models on real antibody developability data. FLAb serves as a guide for protein engineers selecting a model validated on wet lab data for their specific task, and as a resource for researchers benchmarking newly developed protein AI models.

FLAb contains the largest collection of publicly available antibody developability datasets, available on GitHub and Zenodo.

Benchmarks

Overall Developability Prediction Performance

Aggregate performance averaged across all datasets. The quickest way to compare models across diverse antibody formats, assay types, and data sources.

GDPa1 - 246 Clinical Stage IgG Antibodies - Ginkgo Datapoints

Biophysical assay results for 246 IgGs across 10 developability assays including titer, purity, aggregation, thermostability, colloidal stability, hydrophobicity, heparin binding, self-association, polyreactivity, and DLS-kD.

GDPa2.1 - 18 VHH Constructs - Ginkgo Datapoints

Biophysical assay results for 18 VHH (nanobody) constructs in VHH-His and VHH-Fc formats across six developability assays including hydrophobicity, aggregation, colloidal stability, heparin binding, self-association, and thermostability.

GDPa3 - 80 IgG Repertoire Antibodies - Ginkgo Datapoints

Biophysical assay results for 80 IgGs sampled from the Observed Antibody Space (OAS) database across five developability assays including hydrophobicity, aggregation, thermostability, titer, and polyreactivity.

GDPa4 - 160 Bispecific Antibodies - Ginkgo Datapoints

Biophysical assay results for 160 bispecific antibodies built from 65 IgGs selected from GDPa1 across 10 developability assays including thermostability, colloidal stability, hydrophobicity, heparin binding, self-association, and polyreactivity.

AbDesign Database - NaturalAntibody

ELISA binding data for 658 CDR-H3 point mutations across 14 antibody-antigen complexes (7 antigens × 2 antibodies each), with high-resolution wild-type structures and computationally refined mutant models.

AbDesign 2.0 - Point Mutants of Pembrolizumab - NaturalAntibody

Exhaustive mutational scan of the Pembrolizumab-PD1 complex covering 576 heavy chain CDR and framework point mutations, with ELISA binding data and full developability profiles (stability, hydrophobicity, aggregation, polyreactivity) for 346 successfully produced variants.

IgDesign - AbSci

SPR-measured KD data for HCDR3 and HCDR123 variants of seven therapeutic antibodies (Afasevikumab, Bimamagrumab, Eculizumab, Osocimab, Spesolimab, Tesepelumab, Utomilumab) generated by inverse folding across multiple antibody-antigen complexes.

Unlocking De Novo Antibody Design - AbSci

SPR-measured KD data for zero-shot HCDR3 designs of Trastuzumab-HER2 variants, with developability profiles (stability, hydrophobicity, aggregation, polyreactivity) and cross-reactivity data for candidate mAbs.

AlphaBind - A-Alpha Bio

Binding affinity measurements for antibody variants targeting multiple antigens including TIGIT, PD-1 (Pembrolizumab), COVID-19 (VHH72), and HER2 (Trastuzumab), including BLI data and deep mutational scan data.
